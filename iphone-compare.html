Benchtekno Verdict

The iPhone 18 Pro offers a noticeable 15% bump in CPU speed and substantially improved thermal dissipation under sustained gaming loads. If you are coming from an iPhone 16 Pro or older, it is a significant upgrade. However, iPhone 17 Pro users can comfortably skip this generation unless maximum ray-tracing frame rates are essential.
